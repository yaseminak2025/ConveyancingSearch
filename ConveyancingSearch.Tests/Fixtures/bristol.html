Bristol features a mix of Victorian terraces, converted flats and modern apartment blocks in the city centre, which often brings leasehold terms and building safety documentation into the checks.

In parts of Clifton, Redland, Cotham and Lawrence Hill, an Article 4 Direction requires planning permission to convert a family home into a small shared residence. Around Royal York Crescent and Cornwallis Crescent in Clifton Village, a separate direction regulates exterior changes to homes.

Near the waterfront, the Environment Agency has established flood warning areas for the Floating Harbour and the banks of the River Avon, including St Philips Marsh and the Cumberland Basin. Buyers in these areas can expect environmental searches and flood maps to be part of the advisory process.
