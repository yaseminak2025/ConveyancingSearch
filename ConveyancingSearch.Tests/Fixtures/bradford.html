Saltaire, a Victorian model village built for mill workers, is a UNESCO World Heritage Site within Bradford. Most of its stone terraces are listed buildings. An Article 4 direction in Saltaire imposes planning controls on changes such as new windows, doors and roofing. Anyone buying a home in the village needs to know which planning consents were granted for any past alterations.

Article 4 directions also apply in other areas of the city, including parts of Manningham and Little Horton. If a previous owner has replaced windows or painted stonework without the necessary consent, the council has the authority to take enforcement action against the new owner.
