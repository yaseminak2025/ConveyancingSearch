Conveyancing Solicitors in Manchester for Buying, Selling or Remortgaging Property

Buying or selling a property in Manchester can involve local searches, mortgage requirements, contract checks and enquiries about the title. The conveyancing solicitors listed below can manage the legal process, whether you are purchasing a city-centre apartment, moving to a Manchester suburb, selling your home or remortgaging.

Use the quote facility to compare conveyancing costs or contact a property solicitor directly. Ask for a clear written quotation showing the legal fee and any additional expenses, such as searches, Land Registry charges and Stamp Duty Land Tax where applicable.

Your solicitor or licensed conveyancer will review the title, arrange the necessary property searches, raise enquiries and handle the exchange of contracts, completion and registration of the property.

Timescales vary depending on the property, mortgage lender, searches, and the length of the chain, so ask the firm for a realistic estimate based on your transaction.
