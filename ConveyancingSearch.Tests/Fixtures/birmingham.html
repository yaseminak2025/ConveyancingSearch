Birmingham's homes range from city-centre apartments to family houses in Edgbaston, Bournville, Harborne and Sutton Coldfield. In the Jewellery Quarter, a conservation area full of listed former workshops, many new homes are flats created by converting historic buildings, which brings leases and listed building consent into the legal checks.

Whichever kind of home is involved, the work runs through contract checks, property searches, title enquiries and mortgage conditions, then exchange, completion and registration with HM Land Registry. Timescales depend on the property, the lender, the search results and any chain.

A clear quote separates the legal fee from searches, Land Registry fees, bank transfer charges and any Stamp Duty Land Tax, and says whether the fee is fixed and what is charged if a transaction falls through. Firms on Newhall Street and Bristol Road South are among those serving the city. If you want quotes from conveyancers covering Birmingham, please use the form on this page.
