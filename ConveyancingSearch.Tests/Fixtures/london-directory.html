How do I choose a Conveyancing solicitor in London?

When choosing a Conveyancing solicitor you should consider the following:

Comfort Level - Are you comfortable with the solicitor? You will get the most from your solicitor by being open with personal information. Do they seem interested in solving your problem? If your answer is no, then look elsewhere. If you would feel more comfortable with, for example, a female solicitor just ask.

Credentials - Has the Conveyancing solicitor worked on other cases similar to yours, ask for examples?

Cost – Be up front about costs – hourly, no win no fee or flat fee? You must understand this before you instruct, if you want a flat fee then ask.

Convenience - Is the lawyer's office conveniently located? Does it matter? if you like a firm but they are a distance away ask how it will work as many areas of law do not require you to have a face to face meeting and many firms are geared up to work remotely.
